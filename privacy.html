Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Life Dashboard is a private desktop application used only by its developer on their own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar events (read-only, calendar.readonly)
  • Gmail message metadata and short previews in the inbox: sender, subject, time, read/unread (read-only, gmail.readonly)
  • Outlook calendar events and inbox messages via Microsoft Graph (read-only)

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is only used to display an overview to the account owner inside the app.
  • Data is stored only locally on the owner's computer. Access tokens are encrypted with the operating system's protection (Windows DPAPI).
  • The app has no server. No data is sent to, shared with or sold to any third party, and it is not used for advertising or to train any AI models.
  • The app never sends, modifies or deletes e-mail or calendar events.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Life Dashboard's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens and cached data from the computer. Access can also be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
